☀️ Sunday Attendance & Working Salary Mechanism
For eligible employees whose weekly off is Sunday and who work on a Sunday:
  1. Exact Daily Rate: exactDailyRate = baseSalary / totalDaysInMonth.
  2. Sunday Working Salary: sundaySalary = round(sundayPresentUnits * exactDailyRate) (1.0 for full day, 0.5 for half day).
  3. Credited Under Other Allowances: allowanceOther = earnedBaseAllowanceOther + sundaySalary. Added cleanly without salary duplication.

2 Incomplete Month Payroll Proration Formula

To eliminate salary leakage during ongoing months, payslips reflect the actual payable salary for the elapsed period rather than displaying an unearned full monthly salary.

Mathematical Formulation of Period Proration
Let T = Total calendar days in month (28, 29, 30, or 31).
Let D_elapsed = Days elapsed in the payroll period (or T for past completed months).

1. Daily Rate: exactDailyRate = nominalMonthlyBaseSalary / T
2. Eligible Paid Days: eligiblePaidDays = max(0, D_elapsed - absentDays - unpaidSundayDays - (0.5 * halfDays))
3. Prorated Nominal Base: periodNominalBase = round(D_elapsed * exactDailyRate)
4. Attendance Deductions: attendanceDeductions = round((absentDays + unpaidSundayDays + 0.5 * halfDays) * exactDailyRate)
5. Actual Earned Base: earnedBaseSalary = max(0, periodNominalBase - attendanceDeductions)
6. Prorated Allowances: earnedAllowance = round((fullMonthlyAllowance / T) * D_elapsed)
7. Total Deductions: totalDeductions = attendanceDeductions + statutoryDeductions + adhocDeduction
8. Net Take-Home Salary: netSalary = max(0, periodGrossEarnings - totalDeductions)

3 The Standardized 200-Minute Half-Day Rule

The attendance classification engine strictly enforces a symmetrical 200-Minute Threshold across arrival, early departure, and duration:

Attendance Category Trigger Condition Classification & Penalty
Punctual Arrival Arrival ≤ shift start + grace (default ≤ 15 mins). On Time • 1.0 paid day credit.
Grace Period Delay Arrival between grace and 200m cutoff (< 200 mins). Late • 1.0 paid day credit; logged for punctuality.
200-Minute Late Arrival Arrival ≥ Shift Start + 200 minutes (e.g., ≥ 12:50 PM for 09:30 AM shift). Half Day • 0.5 daily rate deducted.
200-Minute Early Departure Clock-out ≤ Shift End − 200 minutes (e.g., ≤ 16:10 PM for 19:30 shift). Half Day • 0.5 daily rate deducted (Vipin Chandra Oct 9).
Short Working Duration Total worked duration < 240 mins (4 hours / 50% of standard shift). Half Day • Short shift penalty.
Missing Departure Punch Clocked in morning, but missed departure punch on a past date. Half Day • Single punch penalty (not full absent).
Missing Arrival Punch Clocked out in evening, but missed arrival punch. Half Day • Single punch penalty.
Unexcused Absence No punch on a past scheduled workday without approved leave. Absent • 1.0 daily rate deducted.

5 The 2-Day Weekly Rule for Weekly Offs & Sundays

To discourage unapproved absenteeism surrounding weekly rest days, the 2-Day Weekly Rule operates as follows:

  1. For every elapsed Sunday, evaluate the preceding 6 calendar days (Monday to Saturday).
  2. Count scheduled working days with absence or leave (full absence = 1.0 unit, half-day = 0.5 unit).
  3. If leavesInWeek ≥ 2.0, and the employee did not work on that Sunday, Sunday is classified as Unpaid Sunday (round(1 * exactDailyRate) deducted).
  4. If the employee worked on that Sunday, Sunday is credited as an attended day and is not penalized.

6 UI Stability & Auto-Close Prevention

To prevent the Monthly Report and payslip preview drawer from auto-closing or resetting after 5–10 seconds:

  • Background Live Re-render Guard: In js/app.js, background biometric synchronization events (db_updated and storage events) are exempted when viewing #admin-reports and #admin-hours-balance.
  • Persistent Modal State: The inspection drawer remains open with drawer.dataset.activeUserId until explicitly closed via the new ✕ Close button.
  • Filter & Pagination Preservation: Selected month, year, search queries, department filters, and current page are retained across all background events.

7 Explicit Payroll & Attendance Acceptance Checklist

HR administrators and Finance auditors must verify that every employee payroll complies with this 10-point checklist:

✔ 1. 200m Late Arrival Check: Clock-in ≥ 200m after shift start marked Half Day.
✔ 2. 200m Early Departure Check: Clock-out ≤ 200m before shift end marked Half Day.
✔ 3. Short Duration Check: Total elapsed work < 240 mins (4 hours) marked Half Day.
✔ 4. Single-Punch Resolution: Past missing in/out punches penalized as Half Day (not full absent).
✔ 5. Incomplete Month Proration: Nominal base scaled strictly by (D_elapsed / T).
✔ 6. Half-Day Deduction: Exact 0.5 × exactDailyRate deducted per occurrence.
✔ 7. Sunday Pay Under Other Allowances: Sunday work credited under Other Allowances without duplicate basic pay.
✔ 8. 2-Day Weekly Rule: Sunday penalized only if preceding weekly leaves ≥ 2.0 units and employee didn't work.
✔ 9. Legacy Concepts Excluded: Overtime, Shift Allowance, and Professional Tax permanently removed.
✔ 10. Inspection Drawer Stability: Monthly report and inspected payslips stay open indefinitely without closing.
